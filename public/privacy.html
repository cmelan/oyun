Çok Kalpli Koruyucu · Privacy

Effective July 26, 2026

Short version: the game has no account, ads, social features, location tracking, or behavioral analytics. Progress and settings stay on the device.

Information used

Game progress, language, and sound preferences are stored locally on the device. We do not receive them.

If a parent opens or buys the optional family unlock, Apple or Google processes the payment. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, device/operating-system information, and purchase information such as the receipt or purchase token so the purchase can be verified and restored. We do not ask for a name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier.

Children

The game is designed for families and does not ask children to provide personal information. Purchases are placed behind a grown-up check. If you believe information was provided unexpectedly, contact us so we can investigate.

Sharing and retention

Purchase information is shared only with the applicable app store and RevenueCat for purchase functionality and restoration. Their retention and security practices are governed by their policies. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can play the complete Meadow chapter without creating an account or making a purchase. You may delete local progress by removing the app. Purchase-history requests are handled through the applicable store and RevenueCat.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: project support.
